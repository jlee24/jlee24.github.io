I feel very lucky to have been part of the cast of "<a href="https://www.facebook.com/events/579978102377771/"> The World of Extreme Happiness</a>," written by Frances Ya-Chu Cowhig and directed by Vineet Gupta. Without revealing too much, it's a story about a migrant worker named Sunny who leaves rural China to work in an urban factory and the transformation she undergoes in pursuit of her vision of success. I'm playing Sunny!
