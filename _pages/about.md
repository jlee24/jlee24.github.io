I received a **B.S. in Computer Science with Honors** from Stanford University in 2019, specializing in Artificial Intelligence and minoring in Feminist, Gender, & Sexuality Studies.
My honors thesis received a [Firestone medal](https://news.stanford.edu/2019/06/20/stanford-undergrads-awarded-theses/), granted to the top 10% of theses across all disciplines.

I also graduated with a **M.S. in Computer Science** from Stanford with a dual specialization in AI and Human-Computer Interaction in 2020.
I received a full merit-based scholarship for my M.S. as a [Siebel Scholar](https://news.stanford.edu/2019/10/03/siebel-scholars-announced/).

My faculty advisers were Maneesh Agrawala and Stefano Ermon. My (now-faculty!) mentors were Ranjay Krishna and Nina Brooks. I am grateful to these mentors.

I am currently at **Google Research** building SKAI, an AI system for detecting damage after natural disasters for humanitarian organizations. Check out our work with the [United Nations World Food Programme](https://innovation.wfp.org/blog/skai-isnt-limit-how-wfp-uses-satellite-imagery-and-machine-learning-emergencies) and [GiveDirectly](https://www.givedirectly.org/hurricane-relief-2022/).

**Research Interests:** Developing AI that is robust for high-risk, high-impact decision-making. Computer vision. In a world with infinite edge cases, how do we create models that adapt to new domains and continually learn?

**Mission:** As a technologist, artist, and human, my mission is to develop technology that empowers people.
