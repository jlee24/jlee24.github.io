I am a **Senior Research Engineer** at **Google Research**, building **AMIE**: a multimodal, conversational, and empathetic [medical intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08448). My mission is to make healthcare more accessible and more robust for patients who fall in the long tail diagnostically and culturally. Previously at Google, I built SKAI, an AI system that detects damage after natural disasters for the [United Nations World Food Programme](https://innovation.wfp.org/blog/skai-isnt-limit-how-wfp-uses-satellite-imagery-and-machine-learning-emergencies) and aided relief efforts for [Hurricane Helene & Milton](https://www.givedirectly.org/hurricane-relief-2022/). 

I received my **B.S. with Honors & Master's in Computer Science** from **Stanford University**, specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Human-Computer Interaction. I had the pleasure of being advised by Stefano Ermon and Maneesh Agrawala. My [research](https://www.pnas.org/doi/abs/10.1073/pnas.2018863118) focused on utilizing computer vision to identify pollution sources from satellite imagery and connect them to health outcomes and policy improvements in the Global South. My work was recognized with a [Firestone medal](https://news.stanford.edu/2019/06/20/stanford-undergrads-awarded-theses/), which is granted to the top 10% of theses across all disciplines, and the [Siebel Scholarship](https://news.stanford.edu/2019/10/03/siebel-scholars-announced/), a merit-based scholarship for the Master's.

**Research Interests:** Developing multimodal, robust AI for high-risk decision-making in healthcare and sustainability. As a child of immigrants, I'm passionate about using technology to help marginalized communities. Outside of research, I love making music, dancing, and hiking!
